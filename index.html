Snow Leopard

The Snow Leopard is a beautiful wild cat found in the mountain ranges of Central and South Asia.

Interesting Facts

  • It lives in cold, mountainous regions.
  • It has thick fur to keep itself warm.
  • Its long tail helps it maintain balance.
  • It is also known as the Ghost of the Mountains.

Habitat

High mountains, rocky slopes and snowy regions.

Food

Wild sheep, goats and other mountain animals.
